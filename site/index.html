Where your vault lives

Storage in an account you control.

Crate uses Cloudflare to sync and store your files. Connect your own account during setup—there is no separate Crate account or storage service.

Read how data is handled ↗
01
Do I need Cloudflare?
Yes. Crate guides you through signing in and choosing an account during setup.
02
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. Sync works in Obsidian mobile, and the reminders web app can send notifications to connected devices.
03
Where are my files stored?
In storage created inside the Cloudflare account you choose. Crate does not add end-to-end encryption.
Early release

Try Crate in a test vault.

Crate is open-source and not yet in the Obsidian community plugin catalog. Start with non-critical notes while the project is still early.
