Open-source Obsidian plugin

Own your Obsidian sync.

Crate keeps your Obsidian notes and attachments synchronized through a backend deployed to your Cloudflare account.

It also turns Markdown tasks into reminders and sends push notifications to your phone through a companion web app.

How Crate works

Crate connects Obsidian to infrastructure deployed in your Cloudflare account, then sends due reminders to the companion app.

Reminders on your phone

Create, complete, and organize reminders from the companion app. Every change is written back to Markdown in your vault.

Privacy and security

Read how data is handled ↗

There is no Crate account or hidden telemetry. These boundaries show where data lives and what the phone app can reach.

Project.md- [ ] Review notesCrate
01

Markdown source

Reminders stay readable and editable as Markdown files in your vault.

Your account
WorkerR2D1
02

Your Cloudflare account

R2 stores vault files. D1 stores sync metadata and device records in the Cloudflare account you connect.

PhoneReminders×Vault sync
03

Scoped phone access

The companion app receives a reminder-only token. It cannot call vault sync or device-management APIs.

Encryption boundary

Crate does not add end-to-end encryption. Your Cloudflare account and Worker can access synced vault contents.

Early release

Install Crate
