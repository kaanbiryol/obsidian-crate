Crate runs on infrastructure you control. There is no Crate account to create.
It is currently a beta; start with a non-critical vault and keep a separate backup.

## Get more from Crate

- [Sync](/docs/features/sync/) — understand what syncs and review pending changes.
- [Reminders](/docs/features/reminders/) — plan your day with tasks stored as Markdown.
- [Reading](/docs/features/reading/) — collect articles, highlight passages, and read offline.
- [Encryption](/docs/features/encryption/) — protect synced content, connect devices, and save your recovery key.
